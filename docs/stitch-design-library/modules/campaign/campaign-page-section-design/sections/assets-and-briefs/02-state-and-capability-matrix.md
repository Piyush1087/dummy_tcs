# Assets and Briefs state/capability matrix - future authoring requirements

**Status:** FROZEN PLACEHOLDER

The future matrix must cover:

- Asset provider/search loading, results, authoritative empty, unavailable, authentication/error, and selection;
- selected Offering preview and confirm capability/busy/error;
- linked Asset read-only source state;
- zero/one/many nested Briefs with supplied Draft/Published or other authorized statuses;
- contextual Asset/Brief actions by capability;
- missing media/optional data and long names;
- mobile sheet and keyboard-open states.

It must prohibit Asset inference, orphan Briefs, Deliverables outside Brief, and Campaign-local editing of Brand Centre data.
