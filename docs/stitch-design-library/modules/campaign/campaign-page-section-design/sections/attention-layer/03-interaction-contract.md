# Attention Layer interaction contract - future authoring requirements

**Status:** FROZEN PLACEHOLDER

Product must freeze:

- exact `View Full Report` behavior and boundary;
- exact authorized Budget edit entry/overlay, fields, validation, save/cancel, and ownership;
- exact actionable CTA targets and their owning surfaces;
- whether Budget edit and Actionable interaction remain shallow on one stable composition or require separate screens;
