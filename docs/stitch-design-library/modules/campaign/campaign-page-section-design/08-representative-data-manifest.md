## Prohibited fixture invention

Do not add:

- performance numbers or trends;
- an AI-written Campaign summary;
- budget committed, remaining, or percentage values;
- creator names, avatars, reach, fit scores, recommendations, or reasons;
- Application records or decisions;
- Collaboration statuses or next actions;
- public sharing URLs, recipients, permissions, or channels.

Later sections must receive their own Product-approved representative-data amendment before generation. A visual placeholder may label data as unavailable; it may not synthesize domain facts.
