# Corrected Campaign Page Stitch grounding

Design only the Brand-side Campaign Page Content Area inside the existing AppShell. Aurora is literal visual authority.

## Stable composition

1. Campaign Header: identity, lifecycle, authoritative summary when available, compact facts, actions.
2. Campaign Attention Layer: performance truth, budget, actionable truth, readiness/remediation.
3. Configuration and remediation where needed.
4. Operational workspace selector and one active body only when backend capability permits.

Operational workspaces are exactly Discovery, Applicants, Collaborations. Desktop uses a vertical left selector and one right body. Mobile uses a compact reachable selector and one body. Reporting is never a workspace.

Lifecycle and readiness are separate. A Published or Live Campaign may be not ready. If it cannot enter operational workspaces, show no disabled/decorative workspace UI.

Assets require explicit Brand Centre selection; no automatic or inferred choice. Brief belongs to exact Asset; Deliverable belongs to exact Brief. Campaign cannot edit Brand Centre data.

Applicants owns Application decisions. Collaborations are independent references, not Campaign lifecycle controls. Do not fabricate AI summaries, performance, budgets, actionables, Discovery recommendations, or Reporting data.
