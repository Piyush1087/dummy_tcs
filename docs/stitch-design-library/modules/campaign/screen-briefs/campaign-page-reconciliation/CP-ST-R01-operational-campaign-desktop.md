# CP-ST-R01 — Operational Campaign, desktop

Model guidance: `GEMINI_3_PRO`. Viewport: 1440×900. Parent: corrected grounding only.

## Representative state

An operational Campaign with backend-permitted workspaces. Discovery is selected; the body truthfully communicates its supplied/unavailable state. Reporting is not a workspace.

## Required composition

1. Production AppShell Content Area.
2. Header: Campaign name; lifecycle status; truthful AI Campaign summary only when authoritative; `View Campaign`, `Edit`, `Share`; one backend-capability-driven lifecycle/activation action (not necessarily a toggle); approximately 3–4 high-value facts.
3. Collapsed Product/Campaign Asset and Brief counts, expandable nested Asset → Brief list, and contextual Add/View actions at the correct ownership layer.
4. Attention Layer: authoritative Campaign Performance, performance unavailable, insufficient data, loading, and local error/Retry variants; budget summary and budget-edit capability; authoritative actionables and no-actionable state.
5. Desktop workspace region: vertical selector left, one active body right; exactly Discovery, Applicants, Collaborations.

Do not fabricate metrics, budget remaining, AI summary, or actionable. Representative display data must be labelled design-only and contract-backed. Preserve lifecycle/readiness separation, backend capabilities, explicit ownership, Aurora, and AppShell. This screen governs primary loading/error, lifecycle/readiness, empty/count, and local progress/error code variants.
