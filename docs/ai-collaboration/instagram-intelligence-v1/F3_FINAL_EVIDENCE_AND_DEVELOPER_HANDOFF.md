## Program identity and accepted scope

Instagram Intelligence V1 is a provider-neutral, source-isolated Brand
Intelligence capability. Settings owns Instagram account lifecycle. Instagram
owns its source-native observations, three derived Instagram Objects and its
consumer projection. The hidden Instagram-to-Brand lane is generation-only and
does not directly mutate canonical/manual Brand truth.

Accepted Product and runtime boundaries remain:

- fixed 30-day V1 window and at most 24 deeply selected posts;
- image inspection, deterministic representative carousel-child inspection,
  and Reel/video cover-or-thumbnail inspection only;
- three Objects: `instagram_content_behavior`, `instagram_audience_profile`,
  and `instagram_organic_performance_profile`;
- Settings-owned connect, reconnect, account-conflict, disconnect and
  delete-data mutations;
- Owner and Campaign Manager refresh; Finance Admin read-only;
- six-peer Brand Centre navigation, one Instagram workspace and route-owned
  media detail;
- no canonical Creator, Collaboration, Offering, Campaign or manual Brand
  mutation.

Campaign objective authority is `AWARENESS / TRUST / ASSETS / ACTION`.
`PULSE / PROOF / PRODUCTION / PUSH` is legacy terminology only.
