## 4. Parent Product clarification — meaningful performance differences

The preflight correctly identifies that sample / coverage / confidence eligibility alone is insufficient to call a tiny delta meaningful.

Accept a versioned Content comparison profile with this refinement:

```text
CREATOR_CONTENT_COMPARISON_PROFILE = v0.1
```

### 4.1 Comparison basis

Compare an eligible cohort against the exact complement of other eligible corpus media using the same provider metric definition, source window and valid denominator basis.

Use the median as the V0 center statistic.

Accepted donor gates remain mandatory:

```text
minimum cohort sample = 3 per side
minimum metric coverage = 50% per side

MEDIUM confidence requires:
sample >= 5
coverage >= 70%
publication dates >= 2

HIGH confidence = unavailable in V0
```

### 4.2 Interaction-response claims

For interaction metrics:

```text
total_interactions
likes
comments
saves
shares
```

A Creator-facing claim that a cohort receives stronger / weaker audience response may be published only from a valid same-media reach-denominated rate.

Raw interaction counts may remain visible as factual detail but must not by themselves drive a `stronger audience response` Content Highlight.

Material rate difference requires BOTH:

```text
absolute delta >= 0.5 percentage points
AND
relative delta >= 20%
```

If either side lacks a valid denominator, the response-rate comparison is inconclusive.

### 4.3 Reach / views descriptive comparisons

`reach` and `views` may be compared as observed count metrics because they are direct distribution outcomes rather than interaction-response rates.

For a Creator-facing descriptive claim such as:

```text
"Reels had higher median reach than the remaining analysed posts."
```

require:

```text
relative median difference >= 20%
```

plus all accepted sample / coverage / basis gates.

Do not describe reach/views differences as stronger engagement or stronger audience response.

### 4.4 Other raw count comparisons

Raw likes/comments/saves/shares/total-interactions count differences may be shown as detailed factual metrics where useful, but do not promote them into a meaningful-performance Highlight unless the corresponding accepted reach-denominated rate rule passes.

### 4.5 Zero baseline

If the comparison baseline is zero:

- do not publish a relative-lift claim;
- do not manufacture infinity / percentage lift;
- an absolute factual difference may appear in detailed Content Performance only when otherwise valid;
- it does not qualify as a meaningful-performance Highlight under V0 solely because the complement is zero.

### 4.6 Language

Use descriptive language:

```text
higher median save rate
lower median share rate
higher median reach
```

Do not use:

```text
statistically significant
caused
performed better because
should create more
```

unless a future Product authority explicitly adds statistical / causal / recommendation semantics.
