# Creator Commercial Setup / Work Preferences V0 — Product Amendment 2

`CREATOR_COMMERCIAL_SETUP_WORK_PREFERENCES_V0_PRODUCT_AMENDMENT_2_UGC_PROJECTS`

STATUS = PRODUCT_FROZEN_AMENDMENT

This amendment extends the frozen Work Preferences V0 register with one additional Creator-controlled commercial preference.

## Open to UGC projects

Add next to the existing gifting / barter preference:

```text
Open to UGC projects
YES / NO
```

Meaning:

- `YES` means the Creator is willing to consider UGC projects where Creator-produced content is delivered for Brand use and publication on the Creator's own social handle is not required.
- `NO` means Creator Shop should not proactively treat UGC opportunities as preferred/suitable for this Creator.

UGC is **not** a separate Deliverable format or a separate Campaign domain.

It remains a use-case variation of the same Campaign → Brief → Deliverable construct, with publishing applicability configured according to the actual opportunity.

For UGC projects, content usage rights are expected by default. Exact usage scope, duration, paid amplification and other rights remain opportunity/agreement-specific and must be explicit in final Campaign/quote/Collaboration terms.

This preference is not a binding commitment:

```text
OPEN_TO_UGC_PROJECTS
!=
AUTOMATIC ACCEPTANCE

OPEN_TO_UGC_PROJECTS
!=
A SEPARATE DELIVERABLE FORMAT

OPEN_TO_UGC_PROJECTS
!=
FINAL USAGE-RIGHTS AGREEMENT
```

A Creator may still reject any specific UGC opportunity.

Future Marketplace/AI Match may use this preference subject to its own Product authority.

Rate Card / Media Kit may project this preference but should not create a second canonical copy.
