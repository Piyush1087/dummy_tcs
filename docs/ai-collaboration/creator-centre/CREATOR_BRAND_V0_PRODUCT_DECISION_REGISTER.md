# Creator Centre — Creator Brand V0 Product Decision Register

`CREATOR_BRAND_V0_PRODUCT_DECISION_REGISTER_V1`

**STATUS = PRODUCT_FROZEN_FOR_V0**

**IMPLEMENTATION = NOT_AUTHORIZED_BY_THIS DOCUMENT ALONE**

This document is Product authority for **Creator Brand V0** inside Creator Centre.

Creator Brand is the Creator-controlled commercial identity / positioning surface. It may use evidence-backed suggestions derived from accepted Creator Intelligence, but source-derived observations and suggestions do not become canonical Creator Brand truth without an explicit authorized Creator action.

Legacy Creator Profile / Media Kit / Instagram profile implementation is reference-only and is not Creator Brand Product, UX, schema, reconciliation or persistence authority.

## 3. Creator Brand V0 purpose

Creator Brand helps the Creator:

> define how they want Brands to understand them as a Creator, while using Creator Shop's observed Intelligence to help them express that identity more clearly.

Creator Brand V0 is neither:

- a read-only AI profile;
- a copy of Instagram profile fields;
- a generic personal-profile editor;
- a Media Kit editor;
- a Portfolio manager;
- a Rate Card / Commercial Setup surface;
- a legal identity / KYC surface;
- an automatic Marketplace / AI Match profile;
- a source-derived identity that silently changes on refresh.

## 4. Canonical ownership model

Freeze:

```text
CREATOR BRAND V0
= CREATOR-CONTROLLED CANONICAL IDENTITY
  ASSISTED BY EVIDENCE-BACKED SUGGESTIONS
```

Creator Brand uses three distinct truth states:

```text
OBSERVED
!=
SUGGESTED
!=
CONFIRMED CREATOR BRAND
```

### 4.1 Observed

Observed source / Content Intelligence may identify evidence-backed patterns such as:

- recurring themes;
- communication patterns;
- creative structures;
- visual execution patterns;
- source-supported language/expression observations where available.

Observed facts remain source-derived Intelligence. They are not canonical Creator Brand identity.

### 4.2 Suggested

Creator Shop may convert sufficiently supported source-derived observations into bounded suggestions for Creator Brand fields.

A suggestion is neither measured fact nor canonical Creator identity.

### 4.3 Confirmed Creator Brand

Only an explicit authorized Creator action may establish or change canonical Creator Brand truth.

```text
SUGGEST
→ CREATOR USES / EDITS / WRITES
→ EXPLICIT CONFIRMATION
→ CANONICAL CREATOR BRAND
```

No suggestion may auto-apply.

---

## 5. Source refresh / reconciliation rule

Freeze:

```text
CONFIRMED CREATOR BRAND
= STABLE UNTIL AN AUTHORIZED CREATOR CHANGES IT
```

A later source refresh may create a new optional suggestion when the accepted evidence rules support one.

It must never silently overwrite a confirmed/edited Creator Brand value.

Example Product meaning:

```text
Current Creator Brand:
Skincare educator

Later observed pattern:
Lifestyle / parenting content becomes materially prominent

Allowed:
"Your recent content has shifted. Would you like to update your positioning?"

Not allowed:
automatically replacing the confirmed positioning
```

V0 does not require automatic reconciliation, conflict resolution or longitudinal identity trend history beyond this non-overwrite rule.

---

## 6. Source lifecycle independence

Creator Brand is Creator-owned business truth, not Instagram-owned truth.

Freeze:

```text
INSTAGRAM / SOURCE DISCONNECT OR FAILURE
!=
DELETE CREATOR BRAND
```

Disconnect, reauthorization failure, provider unavailability or missing current source Intelligence may stop new suggestions or affect their evidence state according to accepted source-lifecycle authority.

It must not erase or rewrite confirmed Creator Brand values.

Creator Settings remains authority for social connection / OAuth / reconnect / disconnect / token lifecycle / purge initiation.

## 7. Creator Brand V0 fields

V0 freezes these Creator-controlled field families:

```text
1. Positioning
2. About / commercial Bio
3. Niches / categories
4. Voice & Personality
5. Visual Identity / palette
6. Languages
```

Exact persistence decomposition / Object-component architecture is not Product authority.

### 7.1 Positioning

One concise Creator-controlled positioning statement answering:

> What should a Brand understand about me quickly?

The Creator may:

```text
write manually
accept an evidence-backed suggestion
edit a suggestion before confirming it
```

V0 owns one current canonical positioning value.

### 7.2 About / commercial Bio

A Creator-controlled Creator Shop commercial identity description.

```text
INSTAGRAM BIO
!=
CREATOR BRAND BIO
```

Instagram/source profile text may inform a suggestion, but it does not silently become Creator Brand truth.

### 7.3 Niches / categories

Creator-selected structured identity categories describing what the Creator is known for / positions themselves around.

Important boundary:

```text
CREATOR BRAND NICHE
!=
COMMERCIAL SETUP PREFERRED BRAND INDUSTRY
```

Identity and commercial willingness remain separate concepts.

Technical implementation should reuse a canonical/shared category/industry vocabulary where semantics genuinely overlap, but must not collapse the two Product meanings into one field.

### 7.4 Voice & Personality

Creator Brand V0 supports a bounded structured voice/personality profile.

Product intent is a small Creator-controlled trait set rather than an opaque unrestricted AI paragraph.

Illustrative traits such as `Educational`, `Warm`, `Direct`, `Playful`, `Aspirational`, `Practical`, `Analytical`, or `Bold` are examples only; the exact controlled vocabulary is technical/Product preflight work and must return for review if no accepted reusable taxonomy exists.

### 7.5 Visual Identity / palette

V0 supports a lightweight Creator-controlled visual identity consisting of:

- bounded visual-style descriptors; and
- an optional Creator-selected palette.

Observed dominant colors / visual execution from Content may support a suggestion but do not automatically become the Creator's chosen palette or visual identity.

### 7.6 Languages

V0 supports Creator-confirmed language/expression information that is commercially useful.

Keep distinct:

```text
CREATOR BRAND LANGUAGES
!= AUDIENCE GEOGRAPHY
!= CREATOR LEGAL / ACCOUNT LOCALE
```

Source/caption observations may suggest language values, but the Creator confirms them.

## 8. Suggestion interaction model

Suggestions appear contextually with the field they can help.

V0 Product behavior:

```text
CURRENT CONFIRMED VALUE
<Creator-controlled value>

OPTIONAL EVIDENCE-BACKED SUGGESTION
<suggestion>

[Use suggestion]
[Edit]
```

If no confirmed value exists:

```text
Suggested for you
<suggestion>

[Use this]
[Edit before using]
[Write my own]
```

V0 does not require:

- an AI Suggestions top-level tab;
- multiple competing suggestions;
- confidence sliders;
- AI-chat editing;
- `reject forever` / permanent suppression policy;
- automatic periodic rewrites.

Exact suggestion evidence / sufficiency / provenance presentation is technical preflight work and must preserve truthful partial/unavailable states.


## 15. Product-level acceptance criteria

Creator Brand V0 is Product-acceptable only when:

1. one canonical Creator Brand identity exists independently of Instagram/source lifecycle;
2. Positioning and commercial Bio are Creator-controlled;
3. Creator may maintain structured niches/categories without conflating them with preferred Brand industries;
4. Creator may maintain a bounded Voice/Personality profile;
5. Creator may maintain lightweight visual identity / optional palette;
6. Creator may maintain confirmed languages;
7. accepted Creator Intelligence may provide evidence-backed suggestions where sufficient;
8. no suggestion becomes canonical without explicit authorized Creator action;
9. Creator may edit a suggestion before confirming it;
10. later source refresh never silently overwrites confirmed Creator Brand truth;
11. missing/unavailable/partial/failing source Intelligence does not fabricate a suggestion;
12. Creator Brand remains distinct from observed Content Intelligence;
13. Creator Brand remains distinct from operational/account/legal identity;
14. Creator Brand remains distinct from Commercial Setup;
15. Creator Brand remains distinct from Portfolio and Media Kit;
16. Owner and Manager may mutate; Assistant is read-only;
17. no downstream consumer automatically gains authority merely because Creator Brand exists;
18. the canonical identity model remains platform-neutral even though Instagram is the V0 suggestion source;
19. no Creator Brand score, automatic recommendation engine or source-driven identity mutation is introduced;
20. integrated implementation preserves accepted Audience / Content / Brand shared-runtime behavior and tenant/subject isolation.
