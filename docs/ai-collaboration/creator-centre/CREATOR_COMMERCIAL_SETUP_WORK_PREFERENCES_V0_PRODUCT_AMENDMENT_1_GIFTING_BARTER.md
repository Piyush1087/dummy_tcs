# Creator Commercial Setup / Work Preferences V0 — Product Amendment 1

`CREATOR_COMMERCIAL_SETUP_WORK_PREFERENCES_V0_PRODUCT_AMENDMENT_1_GIFTING_BARTER`

STATUS = PRODUCT_FROZEN_AMENDMENT

This amendment extends the frozen Work Preferences V0 register with one additional Creator-controlled commercial preference.

## Open to gifting / barter collaborations

Add:

```text
Open to gifting / barter collaborations
YES / NO
```

Meaning:

- `YES` means the Creator is willing to consider opportunities where non-cash Brand consideration such as product/gifting is a material part of the commercial proposition.
- `NO` means Creator Shop should not proactively treat gifting/barter opportunities as preferred/suitable for this Creator.

This is a preference, not a binding commitment and not a new Campaign compensation model.

```text
OPEN_TO_GIFTING_BARTER
!=
AUTOMATIC ACCEPTANCE

OPEN_TO_GIFTING_BARTER
!=
CAMPAIGN COMMERCIAL TERMS
```

A Creator may still reject any specific gifting/barter opportunity.

A specific Campaign's commercial terms remain authoritative when the Creator views/applies to that Campaign.

Future Marketplace/AI Match may use this preference as an advisory/eligibility input subject to its own Product authority.

Rate Card / Media Kit may project this preference but should not create a second canonical copy.

## Authority

This amendment supplements:

`CREATOR_COMMERCIAL_SETUP_WORK_PREFERENCES_V0_PRODUCT_DECISION_REGISTER_V1`

All other frozen Work Preferences decisions remain unchanged.
