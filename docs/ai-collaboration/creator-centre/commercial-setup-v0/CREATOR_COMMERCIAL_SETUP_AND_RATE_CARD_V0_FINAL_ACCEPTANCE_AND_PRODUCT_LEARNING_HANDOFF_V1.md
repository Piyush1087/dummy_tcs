## Authority lessons grounded in this execution

1. Country authority and transfer readiness are different assertions. Amendment 2 allows the current canonical Settings bank country even when transfer readiness is pending/unsupported; the UI does not claim validation, KYC or eligibility.
2. A currency string alone is insufficient. Exact source/version fingerprints prevent old money being relabeled after country-source replacement. Same currency preserves values; cross currency clears them under the explicit frozen contract. No FX or GET mutation.
3. Separate canonical aggregates are compatible with one coherent workspace. Independent fields, migrations, revisions and purge remain explicit while the user navigates two accessible internal sections, not two new workspaces.
4. Reference prices/preferences do not overwrite commercial agreements. Exact format/add-on/payment labels and all nine supremacy statements remain visible; actual populated Campaign/Collaboration rows are unchanged through edits and purge.
5. Source-independent Team authorization must remain explicit. Commercial read/edit actions are separate from platform access and existing domain permissions. Assistant cannot gain a Settings or hidden commercial write path.
