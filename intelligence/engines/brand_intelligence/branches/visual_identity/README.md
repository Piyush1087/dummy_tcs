# Brand Intelligence — Visual Identity

**Status:** PROPOSED FOR SYSTEMS ARCHITECT FREEZE  
**Object authority:** `objects.yaml`

## Purpose

Visual Identity maintains grounded reusable interpretation of Brand visual patterns for Brand Centre, Chat, Campaign Brief generation, Creator Strategy, creator visual guidance, future creative generation and future Brand-consistency evaluation.

## Asset/business-state boundary

Canonical Brand visual assets and approved settings are application/business state, not Visual Identity Intelligence. Visual Identity may reference approved logos/marks, palette, typography and reference imagery but must not duplicate their canonical values or CRUD lifecycle.

Observed visual signals remain Evidence and/or transient candidate state until an accepted Brand/application path promotes them into canonical visual business state where appropriate.

## Descriptive vs prescriptive rule

Creator Shop-derived visual patterns are descriptive by default. They do not become mandatory Brand rules merely because the model observed them repeatedly.

A hard visual constraint requires explicit Brand-authored guidance, explicit Brand confirmation, or another future accepted Product authority.

## Brand Preview compatibility

Brand Preview may continue to use optional `brand_logo` through the existing Identity compatibility path. `visual_style_profile` is not required for Brand Preview, verification or Home.

## Progressive maturity

A partial `visual_style_profile` may become useful after Home when grounded. It may mature asynchronously as approved visual state and evidence improve. No completeness threshold creates an onboarding gate.

## Industry policy

One Visual Identity schema applies across D2C, AI/SaaS, Healthcare and Offline Services. Industry changes relevant evidence and interpretation emphasis, not topology. Healthcare visual interpretation must not infer medical efficacy or professional claims from aesthetics.

## Topology status

Visual Identity may be independently frozen, but the overall Brand Intelligence topology remains provisional until Brand Expression, Audience, Visual Identity and Geography/Locations have been reconciled together and pass a cross-branch Systems Architect review.
