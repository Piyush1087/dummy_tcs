# Brand Intelligence — Audience

**Status:** FROZEN  
**Object authority:** `objects.yaml`

## Purpose

Audience maintains Creator Shop's small durable Brand-level Persona set for Brand Centre, Chat, Creator Strategy and downstream Campaign reasoning.

## Strategic active-set principle

The normal Product target is approximately 2–3 active Personas, but this is not a universal readiness threshold. One strong Persona may remain useful as `PARTIAL`; filler Personas are forbidden.

## Brand Preview boundary

Brand Preview `audience_groups[]` remain bounded hypotheses used only as `CANDIDATE_SEED` and `RECONCILIATION_CONTEXT`. They are not durable Persona identities, raw Evidence or Brand-confirmed truth.

## Campaign targeting boundary

Audience does not own Campaign age/gender filters, affinities, target geography, Campaign objective, creator archetype selection or matching. Campaign targeting may later derive from Brand Audience plus Campaign objective, Offering and Market context.

## Industry and sensitive-inference policy

One Persona schema applies across D2C, AI/SaaS, Healthcare and Offline Services. Industry changes reasoning emphasis, not schema. Demographic context is optional and policy-gated; sensitive inferred traits are forbidden without explicit later authority, with the most conservative policy applied to Healthcare.

## Progressive maturity

Durable Audience completion never blocks Brand Preview, verification or Home. Stage B uses Preview audience groups; durable Personas may become useful after Home and mature asynchronously toward the small strategic active set.

## Topology status

Audience Object authority may be independently frozen, but the overall Brand Intelligence topology remains provisional until Brand Expression, Audience, Visual Identity and Geography/Locations are reconciled together.
